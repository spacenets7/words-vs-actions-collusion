# How the paper audit shaped this project

The applicable findings were incorporated into our controller, monitor views, calibration, analysis and reporting. This is not a claim that we repaired all upstream repositories or independently reproduced every paper. Their checkouts and the frozen experimental configuration remain unchanged. Section 21 of the project plan governs these corrections.

This map covers all **22 confirmed findings** in [the supplied audit](../audit/FINDINGS.md). “Avoided” means the affected component or claim is not used here; it does not mean its upstream defect was fixed. Refuted or unverifiable leads are not reclassified as confirmed errors.

## Remaining limitations

- Independent human agreement/pair/ambiguity adjudication is **pending**. The automatic labels are not a human-reviewed gold standard. Three held-out H cases fail the frozen agreement/implementation gate and were retained.
- The I/H public communication stage is deliberately controlled; this is not evidence of naturally learned concealment. Harmful matching actions alone do not establish an agreement.
- Eight held-out family clusters form a small pilot. Exact binomial rate intervals assume independent episodes; that assumption is limited by family dependence. AUROC and paired intervals cluster by family. No multiplicity-adjusted significance claim or deployment FPR guarantee is made.
- The auditor still makes substantive reasoning errors despite valid JSON, action extraction and citation checks. Those outputs were not corrected or selected away.
- Source artifacts missing from the original papers remain missing; this project does not resolve that lack of access. The report avoids importing unsupported headline gains.

## Evidence and scope of verification

The 23 project tests passed before this graph export; frozen experimental hashes verify unchanged. The completed experiment contains 12 development and 48 held-out episodes, with 240 valid monitor outputs in total. All held-out metrics, including bootstrap values, were independently recomputed from exported records with exact JSON equality and no model/API calls. This is a computational reproduction check, not an independent human review. See [final verification](submission/final-verification.json).

The PNG export reads only saved metrics and the held-out generation counts. It changes no labels, thresholds, prompts, predictions or statistical results.
